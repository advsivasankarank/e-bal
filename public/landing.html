What is e-BAL?

e-BAL is a Financial Reporting Platform designed for Chartered Accountants, Tax Practitioners, Audit Firms, and MSME Consultants.

It imports Trial Balance data from Tally and helps generate professional financial statements quickly, accurately, and efficiently.

Important Note

Tally remains the source of accounting data. e-BAL focuses on reporting, compliance, and financial statement preparation.

💼 Tax Practitioners Tax compliance
